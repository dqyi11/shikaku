    How to play

    • Drag across the grid to draw a rectangle.
    • Every rectangle holds exactly one number.
    • That number is the rectangle's area: a 6 needs 6 squares, like 2×3 or 1×6.
    • Tap a rectangle to erase it. Striped ones are wrong.

    Keyboard: ←↑→↓ move, Enter start and finish, Del erase, Ctrl+Z undo.
